About me

Hi, I'm Cenk. I was born in Ankara in 1979 and spent about 16 years at Turkish Airlines in a variety of roles. My love of aviation brought me to Microsoft Flight Simulator, and I haven't left the cockpit since. From short regional hops to long-haul sectors, I try to fly every flight by real-world procedures.

On this site you'll find my streams, the gear I use, and the training I've put together for people new to virtual flying. Let's fly together.
